Renders any of the 66 Faclon brand and product marks extracted from the Brand Repository, by name.

```jsx
<Icon name="FaclonlogoProperty1Blue" size={40} />
<Icon name="IOSenseProperty1White" size={32} />
```

Read `Icon.d.ts` for the full name list. Covers the Faclon wordmark and monogram
(Black / Blue / White), the product marks (IOSense, IOConnect, Deepsense, Bruce, Bruce AI,
Forge & Foundry, IO Lens), and the ST / GT hardware silhouettes.
Single-colour marks paint with `currentColor`.
