The brand's hand-drawn gradient underline — it goes directly under a title.

```jsx
<h1 className="fl-display-medium">Industry 4.0, delivered</h1>
<SignatureStroke width={340} basePath="../../assets/graphics/" />
```

That is its only sanctioned use. It is **not** a section divider, a horizontal rule, or a decorative flourish elsewhere on the page — using it that way dilutes the one place it means something.

Natural size is 483×9. Scale it to roughly the width of the title it sits under; it stretches horizontally without distorting noticeably.
