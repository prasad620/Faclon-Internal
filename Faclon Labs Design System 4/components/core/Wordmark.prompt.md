The Faclon Labs wordmark. Use the white mark (`light` or `dark`) on brand blue and on any dark surface, `color` on light surfaces.

```jsx
<Wordmark style="color" height={28} basePath="../../assets/logo/" />
<Wordmark style="dark" height={44} />
```

Set `basePath` to the relative route to `assets/logo/` from the page. Never recolour or redraw the mark — only the two supplied SVGs are legitimate.
